Yoga International is an online wellness platform offering yoga classes, guided meditation, breathing exercises, and expert-led programs for practitioners of all experience levels.

Our platform combines structured learning paths, experienced instructors, personalized recommendations, and wellness resources to help members build a consistent yoga practice.

Members receive unlimited access to yoga classes, guided meditations, wellness workshops, personalized practice plans, and new content added every week.

New members can enjoy a free trial with unrestricted access to premium classes and features. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

We offer flexible monthly and annual subscription plans. Annual members enjoy discounted pricing along with full access to all premium content.

Courses provide structured learning over multiple sessions, while challenges encourage you to practice consistently over a set period. Both are included with an active membership.

Browse classes by yoga style, duration, difficulty level, instructor, focus area, body part, energy level, and available equipment to find sessions that fit your goals.
